DevOps Engineer Salary in India 2026: Complete Guide

DevOps engineers are among the highest-paid IT professionals in India. In 2026, the demand for Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD skills continues to outpace supply — making DevOps one of the smartest career investments.

DevOps Salary by Experience Level

  • Fresher (0–1 year): 5–8 LPA
  • Mid-level (2–4 years): 10–18 LPA
  • Senior DevOps/SRE (5+ years): 18–25+ LPA
  • Cloud Architect: 20–35 LPA

Skills That Command Premium Salaries

Kubernetes (CKA certified), Terraform, AWS/Azure cloud platforms, and CI/CD pipeline design are the top salary drivers. DevOps professionals who combine cloud + security (DevSecOps) earn 20–30% more.
