            <p>If you are comparing <strong>SOC Analyst vs Ethical Hacker</strong> careers in India, you are asking the right question before enrolling in <a href="/cyber-security-course-coimbatore">Cyber Security training in Coimbatore</a>.</p>

            <h2>SOC Analyst: Defensive Security</h2>
            <p>SOC (Security Operations Center) analysts monitor alerts, triage incidents, analyze logs in Splunk or SIEM tools, and follow playbooks. The role suits detail-oriented learners who enjoy 24/7 operations rhythms and steady employer demand.</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Core skills:</strong> Log analysis, incident response, threat intelligence basics</li>
                <li><strong>Tools:</strong> Splunk, Wireshark, ticketing systems</li>
                <li><strong>Salary (India):</strong> ₹4–10 LPA entry to mid-level</li>
            </ul>

            <h2>Ethical Hacker / Penetration Tester: Offensive Security</h2>
            <p>Ethical hackers simulate attacks — reconnaissance, exploitation, web app testing with Burp Suite, and reporting vulnerabilities. The role suits curious problem-solvers who enjoy breaking things responsibly.</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Core skills:</strong> VAPT, OWASP Top 10, network pentesting</li>
                <li><strong>Tools:</strong> Kali Linux, Metasploit, Burp Suite, Nmap</li>
                <li><strong>Salary (India):</strong> ₹6–18+ LPA with portfolio and CEH</li>
            </ul>

            <h2>Which should you choose?</h2>
            <p>Many professionals start defensive (SOC) and move offensive (pentest), or combine both. Our <a href="/cyber-security-course-coimbatore">Cyber Security course</a> covers both tracks in modules 3–4 so you can decide during internship-style labs.</p>
